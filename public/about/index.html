ABOUT US

SG Card Bros was started in Nov 2020 and has since established itself as a reputable and industry leader in assisting collectors to send their cards for grading. We pride ourselves on our seamless order tracking and proper order management to bring the best user experience for our submitters.

We are a team of card enthusiasts, avid cards collectors and investors for over 20 years. Individually, we are also reputable and reliable sellers on eBay/Carousell.

Currently we do not have a store or office and we are doing it out of our own free time as this is our passion and we genuinely want to give SG/MY community an avenue to consolidate and send cards to PSA.

We are active on our social media platforms as we showcase our submitters' insane collections and bring you some of the hobby's latest and hottest news. Do check us out and give us a like and follow.
